How it works

Set it once. It hunts while you don't.

No refreshing job boards. No re-reading the same listings. Four steps and the bot takes the night shift.

1

Create a search

Type your keywords — "PHP Developer", "React", anything — pick your countries, and choose a window: last 24 hours, week, or month.

2

Put it on a schedule

Hourly, daily, or weekly sweeps — or hit "Run now" whenever you want a fresh look. Missed schedules catch up automatically.

3

It scans & filters

Every run fetches live listings in real time, drops anything outside your time window, and skips jobs you've already been told about.

4

Fresh matches reach you

A summary lands in Telegram the moment a run finds something new — and the full listings wait in your dashboard, ready to open.

Features

Built like an assistant who never sleeps in.

Everything below already works today — no beta promises, no waitlist.

Saved searches, your rules

Keywords, multiple countries, free-form tags, and a time window — last 24 hours, week, or month. Tune it until the matches feel hand-picked.

Truly hands-off scheduling

Hourly, daily, or weekly triggers with live countdowns. If your machine was off, missed sweeps catch up on the next start — one run, never a pile-up.

Only genuinely new jobs count

A persistent memory of everything already delivered means "new" means new. The same listing never bugs you twice.

Alerts where you actually look

Run summaries arrive as Telegram messages — what was searched, how much was fetched, and how many are new. Zero-email-inbox noise.

Every run, on the record

Full run history with fetched and new counts, delivery status, and a live view while a sweep is in progress. Nothing happens in the dark.

Job details, one click deep

Open any match for the full description, salary or budget, experience level, company or client info — and a straight link to apply.
